Jon's Hot Sauce Recipe 🔥

Ingredients:

  • Habanero peppers
  • Garlic
  • Rice vinegar
  • Honey
  • Lime or lemon juice
  • Salt

Instructions:

  1. Finely chop peppers and garlic
  2. Mix together in a bowl
  3. Store in the fridge for 1-2 days
  4. Enjoy!

Warning: This sauce is hot enough to make your OnlyFans subscription look tame in comparison
